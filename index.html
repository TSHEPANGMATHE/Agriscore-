4. Privacy and your data

What AgriScore collects and why

AgriScore collects sample farm records to calculate a credit-readiness score. This includes:

  • Mobile money transactions (money coming in and going out of your farm account)
  • Cooperative repayment records (instalments you pay to your farming cooperative)
  • Harvest history (what you plant, harvest and sell)
  • A weather-risk indicator for your area

These records are used to create a credit-readiness profile that shows how likely you are to repay a loan, based on your own farming history.

Sharing with lenders

Your information may be shared with participating lenders only with your informed consent and subject to applicable law and the privacy terms below. Lenders will not see your profile unless you switch sharing on. You can switch it off at any time.

How your data is stored

Your choice and the time you made it are saved in this browser only. You can review or change it later in your Profile. This is a demonstration environment, not secure production storage.

Demo data only. No personal information is sent to any external service.

Your rights under POPIA

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) protects your personal information. It means:

  • Informed consent: you must understand and agree to what is collected and why.
  • Purpose limitation: information is used only for the purpose you agreed to — calculating your credit-readiness profile.
  • Data minimisation: only the information needed is collected.
  • Security: reasonable steps are taken to protect your information in this demo.
  • Your rights: you may ask what information is held, request correction, and withdraw consent at any time.

This screen explains AgriScore's privacy approach. It does not, by itself, mean the website is legally compliant. AgriScore is a concept-stage prototype and has no appointed privacy officer, formal certification, or published legal policy.
